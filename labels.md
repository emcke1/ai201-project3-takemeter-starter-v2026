# Label Taxonomy

## detailed
Definition: The post gives at least one concrete constraint someone could plan around, such as number of cast and crew members. Could also include the timeline leading up to the rehearsal and performance.

Example 1: 
I was asked to assistant direct a middle school show, and I’m so excited! We have over 80 cast members, and 40 crew (all middle schoolers), 6 high schoolers (who know what they’re doing), and a production team that also includes people to help with music, choreography, lighting, and general coordination of kids. This is my first time on a production crew. Mostly I’ve been a mom ( my kid is now graduated), a photographer, promo-maker, and printed/online program designer. I’ll take any advice! Should I print out the digital script? Any other organization advice?
Example 2: 
Hi! Our theatre society put on a full length production every year (not musical theatre), however this year we have managed to have 50 people sign up. The issue is since the society was created, the motto has been that everyone who wants to gets to perform. Usually we have around 30 people join so it is easy to double up parts and have a lot of options, in comparison to this year. We are struggling to find a play that we can put on that ensures everyone gets to be on stage and has a good split on gendered parts. We have around a 60/40 split of girls/guys.
We know we will probably have to triple (maybe even have four people) the actors for the main parts but even with this it has been a struggle to find any plays that will work.
Has anyone else had this issue and how did you work around it? Does anyone have any recommendations for plays we could do?

## vague
Definition: The post asks for help or comments based on the experience the author is going through to drive the emotional point of view.
Example 1: 
Hi,
idk if you can help me but here's my situation:
I just signed up for an amateur theater club, it's actually not my first time doing theatre, i did some when i was younger, but i had and still have a problem : due to my autism, my voice is very monotonous. I tried accentuating on some important words, and to like speak high-pitched or low-pitched, or speak louder, but i have no idea about how to do all of that.
I also tried to "express my emotions" like if i was the chr in the situation, but still, it still doesn't sound natural (because the way we express our emotions are social constructs, and us, autistics, are very bad at it)
My backup plan is to see a speech therapist to help me, but hey, i'm not wealthy so i prefer trying by myself at first
Sorry for my (maybe) weird english, i hope someone will be able to help me
Thank you all and have a great dayyy
Example 2: 
I (NB 20) went into college as a computer science and theater double major. I’m in my junior year of college now, and I have been growing increasingly unhappy with computer science. I had a bit of a wake up call with my advisor where I realized that I don’t wanna be stuck doing computer science which I just have grown to absolutely hate and I can’t do it anymore. My one and true passion has always been with theater and it’s time that I really dedicate myself to it.
So, I’m dropping computer science in pursuit of just theater. I know it’s gonna be hard. I know it’s gonna take time. But I genuinely cannot see myself doing anything else and I love theater too much to not make this work.

## Decision rule (hardest boundary: detailed vs. vague)
Borderline post: 


Looking for a full length comedy with literary merit for a large cast (approx 12-15 people). All genres welcome but we would prefer minimum sexual references since it's a pretty conservative community. We want the audience to leave refreshed and thoroughly entertained.

Rule: A post is detailed only if it includes a number or a specific date. Words like "small," "big," or "soon" don't count, so a post with only those is vague.
